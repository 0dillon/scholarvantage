## Platform

## Users

Primary users are accomplished researchers, academics, software engineers, technical innovators, and STEM professionals with significant work in their home countries who need to translate their raw achievements into verifiable, internationally recognized evidence for global talent pathways, prestige fellowships, and cross-border career recognition.

- **Researchers & Academics:** Lecturers, postdoctoral fellows, and principal investigators seeking senior journal publications, peer review credentials, citation visibility, and international society fellowships (IEEE, IET, BCS).
- **Engineers, IT & STEM Professionals:** Systems architects, software engineers, and technical specialists building petition-grade portfolios for EB-1A (Extraordinary Ability), EB-2 NIW (National Interest Waiver), O-1, and UK Global Talent (Tech Nation route).
- **Innovators & Founders:** Tech founders, inventors, and open-source contributors organizing patent documentation, commercialization impact, and institutional recognition.

## Product Purpose

ScholarVantage Global exists to demystify and execute the rigorous evidence-building, profile development, and strategic credentialing required for elite international mobility and peer recognition. Success means a prospective client understands the exact methodology, trusts the founder's direct guidance, and initiates a confidential evaluation via WhatsApp or the structured booking form.

## Positioning

An Elite Global Talent & Scholarly Profile Advisory combining high-touch personal counsel from the founder (John Oluwadarasimi Ojo) with audit-grade evidence curation across both academic (peer review, citations, fellowships) and industrial/technology criteria (patents, system innovations, industry awards). Unlike generic immigration form-fillers or high-volume agencies, ScholarVantage Global operates as a bespoke strategic partner focusing strictly on evidence substance and profile architecture.
